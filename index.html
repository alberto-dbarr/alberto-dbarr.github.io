About

Projects

In progress

A battery free circuit board business card that lights up 21 LEDs when you tap it on a phone.

Details →

This card has no battery. When you hold it against a phone, its antenna picks up energy from the phone's NFC field, and the NFC chip passes that power to a small microcontroller that runs an LED animation. At the same time, the phone reads a link to this website.

Microcontroller
ATtiny816
NFC chip
NXP NTAG I2C Plus
LEDs
21, Charlieplexed
Antenna
PCB coil, ~2.75 µH

What I'm doing

  • Customizing Wilson Harper's open source design in KiCad
  • Ordering the boards fully assembled from JLCPCB
  • Flashing the firmware over UPDI and writing the NFC tag

Built with

KiCad, Arduino (megaTinyCore), NXP TagWriter
